Pixel Futures offers Alpha evaluation accounts in three sizes: $50K, $100K, and $150K.

## Alpha evaluation rules

| Account size | Profit target | Daily loss limit | Max drawdown (end of day) | Max contracts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $50K | $3,000 | None | $2,000 | 3 |
| $100K | $6,000 | None | $3,000 | 6 |
| $150K | $9,000 | None | $4,500 | 9 |

## Consistency rule

Alpha evaluations have a 40% consistency rule. No single trading day can account for more than 40% of your total profit.

Because of this rule, you need at least 3 trading days to pass.

## End-of-day drawdown

Alpha evaluations use an end-of-day trailing max drawdown. Your drawdown level is calculated from your account balance at the end of each trading day.

Intraday swings don't move your drawdown level. You can hold positions through temporary losses with less risk of hitting the limit.

## Pricing

| Account size | Price | Reset price | Activation fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $50K | $209 | $99 | None |
| $100K | $349 | $169 | None |
| $150K | $539 | $259 | None |

There's no time limit on an Alpha evaluation, and you aren't rebilled while it's open.

## Passing the evaluation

To pass, reach the profit target for your account size. You must stay within the max drawdown and meet the consistency rule.

When you pass, you earn a simulated funded account. From there, you can start working toward payouts.

There's no activation fee after you pass.

## Maximum number of accounts

You can have up to three (3) active simulated funded accounts at the same time.

You can combine $50K, $100K, and $150K accounts in any mix, as long as the total number of active simulated funded accounts doesn't exceed three (3).
